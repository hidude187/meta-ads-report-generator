# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a respectful, harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, background, identity, experience level or nationality.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to the project.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the project and the people using it.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming otherwise.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, personal attacks or discriminatory language.
- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Publishing other people's private information without permission.
- Sharing real client data in issues, pull requests or discussions.
- Other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, hide or remove comments, commits, issues and pull requests that do not follow this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently block anyone whose behavior they consider harmful.

## Reporting

To report a problem, contact the maintainer privately through GitHub: https://github.com/hidude187. All reports are reviewed and handled with discretion.
